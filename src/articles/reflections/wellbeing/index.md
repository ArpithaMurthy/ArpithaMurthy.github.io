# Wellbeing

Wellbeing, for me, is not one big achievement. It comes from a few simple things I want to return to regularly, both for my body and my mind.

## Physical wellbeing

### Exercise

Be active. It does not always have to be a structured workout. Walking, stretching, or any movement I enjoy can help me stay consistent.

### Eat well

Choose nutritious food from the many varieties available. I am vegetarian, so I want to find healthy food that I also genuinely like rather than treating eating well as a restriction.

### Sleep enough

Give the body enough time to rest and recover. Sleep is not time taken away from the day. It supports the energy and clarity needed for everything else.

## Mental wellbeing

### Meditation

Create a little space between myself and the constant movement of thoughts. I use Sadhguru’s [Miracle of Mind](https://isha.sadhguru.org/in/en/miracle-of-mind) app to practise meditation.

### Prayer

If I believe that something greater is looking after me, I do not need to carry every worry alone. Prayer can offer trust, perspective, and calm when I cannot control an outcome.

### Family time

Nothing can replace time with family. Nobody knows and loves me in quite the same way. Being present with them gives me a kind of comfort and belonging that is difficult to find elsewhere.

## What I want to remember

Physical and mental wellbeing support each other. I do not always practice all of these perfectly. I want to keep returning to movement, nourishing food, rest, stillness, faith, and the people who make life feel like home.
